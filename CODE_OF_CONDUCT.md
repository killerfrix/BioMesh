# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

As contributors and maintainers of BioMesh, we pledge to make
participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, gender, sexual orientation, disability, appearance,
ethnicity, religion, or experience level.

## Expected standards

Behaviors that contribute to a positive environment:

- Respectful, inclusive language.
- Accepting constructive criticism.
- Focusing on what's best for the project and community.
- Showing empathy toward other contributors.

Unacceptable behaviors:

- Sexualized language or imagery, harassment of any kind.
- Derogatory comments or personal/political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive behavior can be reported by contacting the project
maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated.
