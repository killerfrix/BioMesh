# MIAPPE

**Status: pending full development.**

## What it covers

Minimal checklist for plant phenotyping experiments: Investigation → Study
→ Observation Unit → Variable → Trait → Method.

## When to use it

Directly for the botany domain, instead of reinventing a custom experiment
hierarchy.

## Relationship to other standards

- Darwin Core: a proposed mapping of the `Observation Unit` to
  `Organism`/`Location`, with each measurement session as an `Event`, is in
  [darwin-core.md](darwin-core.md#miappe) (draft).
