# ISA (Investigation-Study-Assay)

**Status: pending full development.**

## What it covers

Of the four standards, the one that best covers instrument + calibration
+ protocol + raw/processed data.

## When to use it

Leading candidate to cover the DIY hardware calibration gap (see
`standards/gaps/` and `standards/calibration/`), since neither Darwin Core
nor MIAPPE address calibration/instrumentation in depth.
