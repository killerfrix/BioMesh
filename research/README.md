# Research

Background research behind two concrete things:

- the rows in [docs/ecosystem-map.md](../docs/ecosystem-map.md) (what was
  reviewed for each project, sources, notes on why each column was filled
  in that way)
- the candidates in [standards/gaps/](../standards/gaps/README.md) (what
  was ruled out and why before confirming a gap)

**Status: empty.** Gets filled in as each ecosystem-map project or gap
candidate is researched — it's not a place for loose notes with no
destination.

Don't confuse this with `standards/mappings/`: `research/` holds the
process and the evidence; `standards/mappings/` holds the already-refined
conclusion.
